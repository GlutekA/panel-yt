Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Panel YT is a personal tool used only by the owner of the connected YouTube channels.

  • What it accesses: with the owner's consent, read-only data of the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.readonly): channel and video metadata, upload/privacy status, statistics and comments.
  • Where it is stored: only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.
  • Sharing: the data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • Changes: the tool cannot modify, upload or delete anything.
  • Revoking access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Panel YT's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
